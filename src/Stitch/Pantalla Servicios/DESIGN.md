## Brand & Style

The brand personality embodies elite software engineering, high-stakes corporate credibility, surgical technical precision, and transformational business impact. It caters to modern enterprise founders, C-suite decision-makers, and forward-thinking operational directors who seek enduring digital evolution rather than transient agency gimmicks. The emotional signature is calm confidence, high velocity, intellectual rigor, and institutional security.

The visual direction harmonizes **Corporate Modernism** with crisp **SaaS High-Density Engineering**. It leverages deep midnight hues for strategic anchors and high-impact hero narratives, coupled with luminous, clinical white and architectural light-gray surfaces for deep analytical work and modular project tracking. Visual elements emphasize fluid geometric arcs, crystalline border definitions, subtle data grids, and glowing cybernetic accents—delivering an experience that feels simultaneously authoritative like premier global management consulting and sharp like modern high-growth developer infrastructure.

## Colors

The color architecture is calibrated to serve dual environments: high-conversion editorial landing surfaces and hyper-focused enterprise client dashboards. 

### Core Palette Application
- **Technological Cyan (`#00C4CC`)**: The primary kinetic color. Reserved for primary conversion hooks, active stage indicators in project timelines, interactive focus rings, and real-time biometric or throughput metrics. It generates immediate visual priority against dark and light canvases alike.
- **Royal Trust Blue (`#0A58A3`)**: The secondary operational color. Conveys stability, governance, enterprise architecture, and secondary action triggers. Used for technical badges, verification pills, and balanced UI controls.
- **Intense Purple (`#4B1D6F`) & Midnight Purple (`#230E38`)**: The tertiary depth layer. Midnight Purple acts as the sovereign hero backdrop, executive modal framing, and VIP status grounding, infusing the software interface with proprietary luxury and visual mass.
- **Graphite Black (`#111114`)**: The dominant typographic neutral and dark console grounding, offering ink-dense contrast without pure black harshness.
- **SaaS Light Surface (`#F4F6F9`) & Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)**: The daylight structural substrate. Dashboards, kanban pipelines, and metric modules sit on `#F4F6F9` with elevated structural cards rendered in `#FFFFFF`.

Chromatic restraint is mandatory: avoid assembling all brand spectrums into single cards. Pair Cyan against Midnight Purple for futuristic impact, or pair Royal Blue against Light SaaS Gray for administrative clarity.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy combines structural authority with mechanical engineering accuracy:

- **Headlines & Editorial Statements (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers geometric clarity with warm, precision-cut apertures. Large scale display types utilize tight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) and heavy weights (`700`–`800`) to project executive decisiveness.
- **Body & Long-form Narrative (`Inter`)**: Deployed for maximal cognitive ergonomics across dense documentation, project milestone logs, and client communications. Set with deliberate line-heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) to prevent eye fatigue across complex dashboard sessions.
- **Data, Metrics & Status Pills (`Space Grotesk`)**: Serves as the quantitative layer. Applied across numerical KPI counters, HX Credit balances, phase indicators, code tokens, and system badges. It injects a high-tech consulting cadence into financial and engineering readouts.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural rhythm follows an intentional 8-point spatial system (`0.25rem`, `0.5rem`, `1rem`, `1.5rem`, `2.5rem`, `4rem`), prioritizing intentional architectural breathing room over clutter.

### Layout Philosophy & Grid Strategy
- **Public Corporate Site**: Standard 12-column responsive fluid grid with a maximum content containment bounding-box of `1280px`. Generous vertical segment pads (`5rem` to `8rem`) establish deliberate pacing across problem/solution decks and technological methodologies.
- **Client Portal (HummingX Core)**: Multi-pane layout architecture. A fixed `260px` dense vertical navigation rail anchors the left boundary on desktop screens, releasing the remaining viewport to a fluid analytical canvas (`100% - 260px`).
- **Responsive Adaptations**:
  - **Desktop (`>= 1024px`)**: Full 12-column structure; expansive sidebars; split dashboard viewports with real-time milestone timelines parallel to financial HX Credit allocations.
  - **Tablet (`768px – 1023px`)**: 8-column layout; navigation collapses to an elevated floating bar or iconography rail; dual-column metric cards stack into consolidated blocks.
  - **Mobile (`< 768px`)**: Single-column vertical stream; project phase bars morph into vertical stepped diagrams; critical action nodes (Dashboard, Project Stage, HX Credits) anchor into an elevated glass-bottom dock.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses physical-grade tonal layering alongside ambient luminosity rather than heavy drop-shadows.

### Depth Systems
- **Surface Elevation 0 (Base)**: `#F4F6F9` in light dashboard views; `#230E38` or `#111114` in high-impact executive canvases.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Interactive Panels & Cards)**: Pure White `#FFFFFF` on light backgrounds; `#2A1242` on Midnight Purple backdrops. Reinforced with a microscopic hairline border: `1px solid rgba(17, 17, 20, 0.06)` on light surfaces, and `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` on dark consoles.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Dropdowns, Floating Overlays, Tooltips)**: Elevated with subtle ambient diffusion: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(17, 17, 20, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(17, 17, 20, 0.03)`.
- **Glassmorphic Floating Bars**: Used for fixed navigation headers and sticky action banners. Styled with `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` (or `rgba(35, 14, 56, 0.85)` in dark themes), supported by `backdrop-filter: blur(16px) saturate(180%)`.
- **Cybernetic Glow (Brand Signature)**: Interactive CTA buttons, live progress nodes, and HX Credit hero components utilize a low-opacity cyan radial halo: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px 0 rgba(0, 196, 204, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The design system standardizes on a **Rounded Level 2** profile, enforcing contemporary refinement without playful softness:

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / `8px`)**: Applied to all core utility controls, form inputs, button elements, code snippets, status badges, and tab selectors.
- **Large Radius (`1rem` / `16px`)**: Applied to executive metric cards, onboarding kits, modal dialogs, and service solution containers.
- **Extra-Large Radius (`1.5rem` / `24px`)**: Applied selectively to primary landing page hero enclosures and structural dashboard outer frames.
- **Pill Form Factor (`9999px`)**: Exclusively reserved for status chips, micro tags (`Space Grotesk` metadata), and interactive segmented pills.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary CTA**: Solid Technological Cyan (`#00C4CC`) background with Graphite Black (`#111114`) text, font weight `600`, radius `8px`. Hover adds an elevated state with `rgba(0, 196, 204, 0.35)` ambient glow and a `-1px` vertical translation.
- **Secondary Corporate Action**: Royal Blue (`#0A58A3`) background with Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) text. Hover dims to `#084988`.
- **Ghost / Outline**: Transparent fill, `1px solid rgba(17, 17, 20, 0.15)` border, `#111114` text. On dark midnight surfaces, uses `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` with `#FFFFFF` text.

### Badges, Status Indicators & Membership Tiers
- Built using `Space Grotesk` uppercase text (`11px`, letter-spacing `0.08em`).
- **HummingX Member**: Slate Gray base (`rgba(100, 116, 139, 0.1)`) with neutral ink.
- **HummingX Partner**: Royal Blue base (`rgba(10, 88, 163, 0.1)`) with `#0A58A3` ink and verified shield icon.
- **HummingX VIP**: Deep Midnight Purple tint (`rgba(75, 29, 111, 0.12)`) paired with `#00C4CC` cyan micro-border and cyan icon accents.

### Cards & Analytical Panels
- Standard cards feature Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) backgrounds, `16px` radius, hairline border (`#E2E8F0`), and internal padding of `24px` (`1.5rem`).
- Hover effects never rely on harsh movements; they feature a subtle cyan border highlight (`border-color: rgba(0, 196, 204, 0.6)`) and a gentle rise.

### Project Timeline & Progress Trackers
- Horizontal stepper on desktop; vertical chain on mobile.
- **Completed Nodes**: Filled Royal Blue (`#0A58A3`) with white checkmarks.
- **In-Progress Stage**: Technological Cyan (`#00C4CC`) pulse ring with live progress percentage in `Space Grotesk`.
- **Pending Stages**: Dashed neutral border (`rgba(17, 17, 20, 0.2)`) with subdued labels.

### HX Credit Balance & Referral Cards
- Dark Mode Card variant: Midnight Purple (`#230E38`) background with radial cyan glow in the top-right quadrant.
- Quantitative display: Bold `Space Grotesk` numerals (`32px`–`40px`) paired with a technological cyan badge stating `Créditos HX Disponibles`.
- Integrated progress arc communicating next redemption eligibility tiers.

### Form Inputs & Data Fields
- Background: `#FFFFFF`; border: `1.5px solid #E2E8F0`; radius: `8px`; height: `44px`; padding: `0 14px`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#00C4CC` with an outline ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(0, 196, 204, 0.18)`. Placeholder text in subdued cool gray (`#94A3B8`).

### Accordions (FAQ & Scope Breakdown)
- Seamless flat borders with clean padding transitions. The active expanded state reveals an unobtrusive left accent border in `#00C4CC` (`3px solid`).
